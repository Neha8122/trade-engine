Raft Replication LLD

Three machines keep the same ordered log of commands. Each one feeds its log, in order, into its own copy of the order book. Because the book is deterministic, all three books stay identical, and if the leader dies a follower takes over with nothing lost.

4 · The two messages and the rules that make it safe

RequestVote: "vote for me in term T"

Sent bya candidate, to every other node
Carriesterm, candidateId, lastLogIndex, lastLogTerm
Grant the vote only if(1) its term ≥ mine, (2) I haven't voted for someone else this term, and (3) its log is at least as up to date as mine: higher last term, or same last term and at least as long
Why rule 3a node missing committed entries can never win, so a new leader always has every committed order (leader completeness)

AppendEntries: "here are entries after index P"

Sent bythe leader: new entries, or empty as a heartbeat every ~50 ms
Carriesterm, prevLogIndex, prevLogTerm, entries[], leaderCommit
Follower accepts only ifit has an entry at prevLogIndex with prevLogTerm; if not, it says no and the leader retries from one index earlier
On a conflictthe follower deletes its conflicting entry and everything after it, then appends the leader's. Only uncommitted entries can ever be deleted

Three rules people get wrong (interviewers ask about these)

Persist before replyingcurrentTerm, votedFor and new log entries are written to disk and fsynced before a node answers. Otherwise a node could crash, forget its vote, and vote twice in one term.
Only count replicas for current-term entriesa new leader never marks an old-term entry committed just because a majority has it (paper Figure 8). It commits a no-op in its own term first, which commits everything before it.
Apply only committed entries, in orderlastApplied walks up to commitIndex one entry at a time. An order is acked to the client only after it's applied on the leader.

6 · How we'll prove it's correct

Scenario tests

Election3 nodes elect exactly one leader; kill it, a new one is elected in a higher term
Replicationproposed commands reach every node's log in the same order
Slow followera follower that missed entries catches up (nextIndex backs off)
Conflicting logan old leader's uncommitted entries are overwritten
Partitionthe minority side can't commit; after healing it rejoins and converges
Crash + restartnode reloads term, vote and log from storage and doesn't vote twice

Randomised simulation (the strong one)

Thousands of seeded runs with random message loss, delays, partitions and crashes. After every step, check Raft's safety properties:

Election safetynever two leaders in the same term
Log matchingsame index + same term ⇒ same entry and same history before it
State machine safetyno two nodes apply different commands at the same index
No acked lossevery order acked to a client is in every future leader's log
Same booksevery node's OrderBook emits identical events
