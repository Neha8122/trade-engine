Trade Engine HLD

The whiteboard version: draw the system view first, walk the numbered order flow, then zoom into one shard leader when the interviewer asks how you hit the latency target.

System view

  1. Client sends a binary NewOrder over its TCP session.
  2. Gateway decodes, runs risk checks, finds the symbol's shard, forwards to that shard's leader.
  3. Sequencer stamps a sequence number and timestamp, appends to the log.
  4. Replicate in batches to both followers; committed once a majority has it.
  5. Matching engine matches against the in-memory book or rests the order.
  6. Publish the exec report back via the gateway, book updates out on multicast.
  7. Client gets its ack or fill. Followers apply the same command to their standby books.
  8. Archiver streams committed trades to Kafka and the trades DB for clearing and audit.
